1. "[Best Yoga Blocks and Straps for Beginners in 2026](/fitness/buying-guides/best-yoga-blocks-and-straps-for-beginners/)" (Sports — Yoga)

A props buying guide that follows up on the site's existing yoga mats post (which had teased blocks and straps as a future topic). Covers the EVA foam vs. cork material tradeoff for blocks (foam is lighter and cheaper but compresses over time; cork holds its shape and grips the floor better but is heavier and firmer), the standard 4x6x9in block sizing that makes material the real decision rather than size, D-ring vs. cinch strap buckles (D-ring is easier to adjust precisely; cinch is faster one-handed but a cheap plastic cinch can slip), and strap length sizing by height (6ft/8ft/10ft). Picks: the Gaiam Essentials block+strap combo (~$15-20, lowest-risk starter), Trideer cork blocks 2-pack (~$30-38, durability upgrade), Alo Uplifting block (~$34 each, comfort/grip upgrade), and the Hugger Mugger 8ft cotton cinch strap (~$17-19).

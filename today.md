A new subcategory within the existing Cycling vertical (alongside road bikes, mountain bikes, helmets, locks, and lights), covering how to transport a bike by car rather than gear for riding it. Explains the three mount types — hitch (most stable, needs a hitch receiver), trunk (cheapest, no hitch needed, more sway and paint-rub risk), and roof (frees up the trunk/hitch but requires lifting the bike overhead and watching garage clearance) — plus frame-clamp vs. wheel-tray contact, e-bike weight ratings, and hitch receiver class (1.25" vs. 2"). Picks: the Allen Sports Deluxe 2-Bike hitch rack (~$100-$130, budget clamp-style), the Yakima FullBack 2-Bike trunk rack (~$299, no hitch required), the Yakima HighRoad roof rack (~$230/bike, wheel-on design), and the Kuat Transfer V2 2-Bike hitch rack (~$549, premium wheel-tray platform safe for carbon/e-bikes).

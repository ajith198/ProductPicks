1. "[Best Climbing Harnesses for Beginner Gym and Outdoor Climbers in 2026](/climbing/buying-guides/best-climbing-harnesses-for-beginners/)" (Sports — Climbing)

A first-harness buying guide to pair with the site's existing beginner climbing shoes post. Covers why adjustability (independently adjustable waistbelt/leg loops) matters more than ultralight weight for a first harness, the difference between the load-bearing belay loop and the non-load-bearing gear loops, why CE/UIAA certification is a non-negotiable baseline rather than a comparison spec, and how to check fit with a hang test (belay loop at the navel, waistbelt above the hips) rather than relying on a size chart alone. Picks: the Black Diamond Momentum (~$65-80, most comfortable default), the Petzl Corax (~$70-85, widest adjustable fit range for bigger frames/cold-layer climbing/sharing), the Black Diamond Solution (~$90-100, lighter step-up for frequent sport/gym climbers), and the Camp Energy CR 3 (~$50-60, budget pick).
